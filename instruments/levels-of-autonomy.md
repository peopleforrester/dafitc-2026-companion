# Levels of autonomy

Five capability levels, and the human control posture each one implies.

**Most of the value is not at the top.**

---

## The levels

**Level 1. Assistant**
Ask and answer. No access to your systems.
Control posture: **human in the loop**

**Level 2. Retrieval-grounded**
Answers from your cleared corpus, with citations.
Control posture: **human in the loop**

**Level 3. Tool-using**
Calls systems under supervision, one action at a time.
Control posture: **human in the loop, per action**

**Level 4. Read-only autonomous**
Runs continuously, observes and briefs, changes nothing.
Control posture: **human on the loop**

**Level 5. Action-taking**
Alters systems of record.
Control posture: **human out of the loop**

---

## The line that matters

**Level 4 is the highest value per unit of risk.** Continuous work, nothing
committed to an authoritative record.

When risk is high, you stay **on** the loop and never **out** of it.

Level 5 has the highest barrier. It requires everything below it proven first.
Most organizations try to jump straight to it, and that is the pattern behind the
cancellation forecast.

---

## Where this comes from

The five levels are our synthesis. They are reduced from a two-axis design space
that separates **autonomy**, how independently a system acts, from **agency**,
how far its writes reach, with five levels on each axis. High autonomy paired
with low agency is the coordinate that captures most of the value while making a
consequential wrong action structurally impossible. [S17]

The control posture is the Department's own language. Air Force doctrine directs
that commanders calibrate the level of autonomy to mission risk, and that AI
augments Airmen rather than replacing them. [S16]

Keep this scale separate from the [proficiency code
key](../reference/proficiency-code-key.md). One measures how much supervision a
*person* needs on a task. This one measures how much independence a *system* has
been granted. An operator at proficiency 3 supervising a level 4 read-only agent
is a coherent position, and merging the two rulers is how you end up measuring a
person with a machine's scale.
