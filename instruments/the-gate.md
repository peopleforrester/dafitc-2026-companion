# The gate

Seven questions. Run a candidate workflow through them before any AI work
starts. **If it fails two, it is not your beachhead.** Pick a different one.

This applies to every workflow, not only the first.

## Why these seven

The failure mode in the evidence is not weak models. It is generic tools bolted
onto workflows nobody redesigned. [S13]

Buying or partnering succeeded roughly twice as often as building internally,
and back-office work returned more than the sales and marketing pilots that took
most of the budget. [S13]

Gartner forecasts more than 40 percent of agentic projects cancelled by the end
of 2027, on cost, unclear value, or inadequate risk controls. [S14]

And the federal version of the same lesson: agencies are not systematically
capturing what they learn from AI programs, and some could not use their own
model outputs because data rights were never negotiated at award. [S15]

What worked looks different. PANDA, Air Force predictive aircraft maintenance,
works because the workflow is bounded, the output is verifiable, and a maintainer
stays in the loop. [S21]

The gate is our synthesis. Ranking candidates on value and feasibility is
standard practice; organizational readiness is ours.
