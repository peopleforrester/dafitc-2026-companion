# Official documents

Your own authoritative sources, so you do not have to take a vendor's word for
anything here.

Several `.mil` hosts return 403 to automated link checkers while opening
normally in a browser. Where that is the case it is noted. Those URLs are real
and correctly formed.

## Training and qualification

### DAFMAN 36-2689, Training Program (31 March 2023)

`https://static.e-publishing.af.mil/production/1/af_a1/publication/dafman36-2689/dafman36-2689.pdf`

This is the governing authority for the Proficiency Code Key, core-task
identification, on-the-job training, and the AF Form 623 training record. It is
the document behind the method in [assess, roadmap,
reassess](../instruments/assess-roadmap-reassess.md).

**It replaced AFI 36-2201, Air Force Training Program, which is rescinded.**
Copies of the 2010 AFI still circulate on unofficial mirrors. Do not cite it.

### Your own CFETP

Search by "CFETP" plus your AFSC at `https://www.e-publishing.af.mil/`

The Proficiency Code Key and the core-task list live in the attachments of each
Career Field Education and Training Plan. That is where you map any AI
competency claim onto codes a supervisor already signs against.

### AF Form 623, On-the-Job Training Record

Cited by form number deliberately. Every URL that surfaces for this form is a
third-party form-filler mirror rather than an official one, so use the
e-Publishing forms search instead of a link. Note that both "AF Form 623" and
"DAF Form 623" appear in circulation.

### DoDM 8140.03, Cyberspace Workforce Qualification and Management Program (February 2023)

`https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodm/814003p.pdf`

DoD Cyber Workforce Framework work-role catalog:
`https://www.cyber.mil/cw/dcwf/`

The strongest available precedent that demonstrated competency rather than a
certificate is departmental policy and not a vendor opinion. It is a mandatory,
competency-based qualification model: foundational qualification within nine
months, demonstrated on-the-job readiness within twelve. It already contains
data and AI work roles.

## AI doctrine and strategy

### Air Force Doctrine Note 25-1, Artificial Intelligence (8 April 2025)

`https://www.doctrine.af.mil/Operational-Level-Doctrine/AFDN-25-1-Artificial-Intelligence/`

The Air Force's first doctrinal statement on AI, built around human-machine
teaming that amplifies rather than replaces Airmen, and around commanders
calibrating autonomy to mission risk. This is the authority behind [levels of
autonomy](../instruments/levels-of-autonomy.md), in your doctrine rather than
our framing.

### DAF Data Strategy and DAF AI Strategy (endorsed 17 April 2026)

News release:
`https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4467267/daf-releases-data-ai-strategies-to-accelerate-military-dominance/`

One of the strategy's building blocks is cultivating access to an AI-literate
workforce.

The strategy PDF is deliberately not linked here. The copy that surfaces in
search carries PREDECISIONAL and CUI markings on its first page, which is
inconsistent with a public release and may be a residual cover page. Navigate
from the news release rather than from a search result, and check the markings
before you forward it.

### Artificial Intelligence Strategy for the Department of War (12 January 2026)

`https://media.defense.gov/2026/Jan/12/2003855671/-1/-1/0/ARTIFICIAL-INTELLIGENCE-STRATEGY-FOR-THE-DEPARTMENT-OF-WAR.PDF`

The current department-level AI strategy. It displaces the November 2023 DoD
Data, Analytics and AI Adoption Strategy.

Note the department renamed to Department of War, so older `defense.gov` links
now redirect to `war.gov`. Check any DoD citation you are carrying forward.

### DoD AI Ethical Principles (adopted 21 February 2020)

`https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/release/article/2091996/dod-adopts-ethical-principles-for-artificial-intelligence/`

Implementing memo:
`https://media.defense.gov/2021/May/27/2002730593/-1/-1/0/IMPLEMENTING-RESPONSIBLE-ARTIFICIAL-INTELLIGENCE-IN-THE-DEPARTMENT-OF-DEFENSE.PDF`

Responsible, Equitable, Traceable, Reliable, Governable.

Infrastructure-level enforcement is an implementation of **Governable** and
**Traceable**. Saying it in that vocabulary is stronger than saying it in ours.

### DoDD 3000.09, Autonomy in Weapon Systems (25 January 2023)

`https://www.esd.whs.mil/portals/54/documents/dd/issuances/dodd/300009p.pdf`

Where the upper end of the autonomy scale stops being a productivity question
and becomes a senior-review requirement. The 2023 version replaced the 2012
original and was characterised as a clarification rather than a policy shift.

## Risk frameworks

### NIST AI 100-1, AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (January 2023)

`https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf`

Hub: `https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework`

GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE is the federal common language for the enforcement
argument. GOVERN explicitly covers workforce competency and role clarity.

### NIST AI 600-1, Generative AI Profile (July 2024)

`https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf`

Names the generative-AI-specific risks, including confabulation, information
integrity and data leakage, that belong in [the
gate](../instruments/the-gate.md) when you score a candidate workflow.

## Two currency warnings

**NIPRGPT was sunset on 31 December 2025**, following the launch of GenAI.mil on
9 December 2025. Any material naming NIPRGPT as the DAF generative AI platform
is out of date.

**Some career fields are migrating off the numeric proficiency scale.** A
behavioural-statement coding system using P for performance, K for knowledge and
pk is replacing the 1-2-3-4 and A-B-C-D key in some CFETPs. The [code
key](proficiency-code-key.md) here reproduces the numeric generation. Check which
one your own CFETP uses before you map anything onto it.

## Deliberately not cited

**AFH 36-2235, Information for Designers of Instructional Systems**, and
**AFMAN 36-2234, Instructional System Development**. Only third-party mirrors
surfaced, and it could not be confirmed that either is still a live publication.
A rescinded handbook in a leave-behind is worse than no citation.

**DAFI 36-2670, Total Force Development.** The parent development instruction,
but hollowed out by interim changes. Training moved to DAFMAN 36-2689. Cite the
children rather than the parent.

**DoD Responsible AI Strategy and Implementation Pathway (June 2022).** No
canonical URL could be confirmed. The citation is complete enough to find it.
