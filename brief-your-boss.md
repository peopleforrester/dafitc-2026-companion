# Brief your boss

One page. Forward it, or read it out.

---

**The requirement is already set.** The DAF AI Talent Plan, approved April 2026,
shifts the standard from course completion to proven skills and competencies. It
sets a hard target of AI Competitive by 2027.

**No directive says how to measure it.** That is the gap.

**We already know how.** Every task in every Career Field Education and Training
Plan carries a proficiency code, graded by how much supervision the work still
needs. Level 3 is not "did it well". It is *needs only a spot check of completed
work*, and it is labelled **Competent**. For on-the-job training the standard is
go/no-go: can the person do it **without assistance**, to local standards for
accuracy, timeliness and correct procedures.

We have applied that instrument to every other skill in the force for decades.
The question is why AI would be the one skill we take on self-report.

**Self-report does not work here.** In a controlled trial, experienced developers
believed AI made them 20 percent faster. They were measured 19 percent slower.
Economists predicted 39 percent faster and machine learning researchers 38
percent. Everyone was wrong in the same direction.

---

## What we are proposing

**Three stages, not one.** Knowledge training proven by completion. General skill
proven by assessment. Context-specific outcomes proven by a capstone in the
person's own environment, with their own tools, measured against the workflow's
existing measures.

Knowledge is not skill. Skill is not an outcome.

**Measure the delta, not the certificate.** Assess a named task against the
published standard, deliver the path, then reassess the same task against the
same standard. Report the movement. Assessments run about fifteen minutes at each
end.

**Pick the workflow deliberately.** Seven questions decide whether a workflow
qualifies for AI at all. If it fails two, it is not the beachhead. Most failed AI
programmes are a new tool dropped into a process nobody redesigned, not a weak
model.

**Enforce guardrails in infrastructure.** A constraint written into a system
prompt is a request, not a control. Recent peer-reviewed work put jailbreak
success across model combinations at 97.14 percent. The Department already proved
the alternative pattern on itself with continuous authorization: enforce it in
the infrastructure, log it, and let an authorizing official read the record.

---

## What we are asking for

1. **One workflow** run through the seven-question gate, chosen deliberately.
2. **A named task and a baseline** for the people who own that workflow, using
   our own proficiency codes.
3. **A capstone in our environment**, not a vendor sandbox.
4. **A reassessment** against the same instrument, so the result is a measurement
   rather than a claim.

---

## What this costs us if we do nothing

We keep buying training we cannot evaluate, and we keep reporting completion as
though it were competency. When someone asks whether the force got better at AI,
we will have graduation rates and no answer.
